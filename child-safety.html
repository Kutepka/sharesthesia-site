Child safety standards

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version

Sharesthesia is for adults, 18 and over. Child sexual abuse and exploitation of any kind is prohibited here, in any form, and we act on it: the account goes, the material is preserved for the authorities, and we report it. Anyone can report a profile or a photo from inside the app, or by email.

What is prohibited

Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers a minor is prohibited on Sharesthesia. That includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form; sexual content involving or depicting anyone under 18; grooming, meaning any attempt to build contact with a minor for sexual purposes; sextortion, meaning threats to share intimate images; and any use of the app to arrange or encourage the abuse of a child. It also includes minors using the app at all: Sharesthesia is for people 18 and older, and an account that appears to belong to a minor is removed.

How we keep minors out and abuse off

Every account confirms being 18 or older at sign-up, and the app is listed for adults only on Google Play, including the setting that keeps it from accounts Google knows to be under 18. Every photo is checked automatically before anyone else can see it, and sexual or violent images never reach a profile. There is no chat inside the app, so no private messaging between people who haven't both chosen to connect; links to outside messengers open only after a mutual ping, and blocking someone cuts every connection at once.

How to report

In the app, open any profile and choose Report. The reasons include Under 18, and you can add details. Reports are read by a person. You can also write to sharesthesia@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety"; you don't need an account to do that.

What happens to a report

A report that involves a minor or any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation is handled first. The account is suspended while we look, and removed when the report is confirmed. Material that may be CSAM is removed from the app, preserved for law enforcement, and reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where it applies and the police in the Republic of Moldova. We cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement in any country.

Our commitments

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy. We keep these standards public, review them when the app changes, and we don't make exceptions to them for anyone.

Contact

Child safety contact: sharesthesia@gmail.com. The person behind the app, Serghei, answers these messages himself.
