Delete your account

Two ways. Both remove everything.

In the app

Open You → Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm. Your account and everything attached to it are deleted right away. You can make a new account later with the same Google account; it starts from zero.

By email

If you can't open the app, write to sharesthesia@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my account and your username. The account is deleted within 7 days and you get a reply when it's done. Writing from the same address is how we know it's you; we won't ask for anything else.

What is deleted

Your profile, photos, items and favourites, every play the Listener counted, your week, skills and levels, Spins, Runs and Sparks, pings and mutuals, notices, quests, invites, blocks, push tokens, the copy of your Steam library, and the sign-in account itself with its sessions.

What stays for a while

Reports you made stay, with your name removed. Server logs keep your old user id until they expire on their own. Catalogue lookups (artist, track and game names) were never stored under you in the first place.

Removing some things without deleting the account

You don't have to delete everything to delete something. In Settings you can disconnect Steam or Last.fm, turn the Listener or mobile games off, untick any game, remove any photo or item from your profile, and hide your week. The privacy policy lists all of it.
