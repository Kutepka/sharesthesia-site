Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. One account per person, tied to your own Google account. Don't create an account for someone else, and don't pretend to be someone you are not: the photos are of you, the taste is yours.

How the app works

Your profile is built from what you listen to and play. Time spent becomes points: Spins for minutes of music, Runs for full hours in a game, and Sparks for quiet signals, quests and invited friends. Points are spent inside the app, mostly on the Sonar, which finds you one person at a time who shares something real with you. There is no chat: if two people ping each other, they see each other's links and talk wherever they already talk.

Points have no money value. They can't be bought, sold, traded, transferred or cashed out, and they exist only inside the app. We may change how they are earned and spent as the app grows; we'll say so in the app when we do.

The rules

Playing music to an empty room, modified apps, bots, scripts, emulators and anything else meant to inflate your minutes, hours or points is cheating. We check for it, and we may reset points, remove levels or close accounts that do it.
  • Be decent. No harassment, threats, hate, or sexual content aimed at anyone. The Sonar introduces people; what happens after a mutual ping is between you, and the normal rules of being a human being apply.
  • Your photos are of you. No nudity, no violence, no other people's faces, no minors. Every photo is checked automatically before it is shown, and declined photos never appear.
  • Don't break the app. No scraping, no automated access, no probing the server, no reverse engineering beyond what the law allows, no interfering with other people's accounts.
  • Your links are yours. The "Find me on" links you add should lead to you. Don't use them to advertise, sell or redirect people somewhere else.

If you break these rules, we may remove content, reset points, suspend or delete your account. Where it is safe to do so we'll tell you why. Anyone can report a profile from inside the app, and reports are read by a person.
